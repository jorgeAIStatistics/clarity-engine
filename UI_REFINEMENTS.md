# Clarity professional UI pass

This pass preserves the 2026.09-launch.2 scientific and production safeguards while refining the presentation.

## Compound page hierarchy
1. Compound identity, verdict, unique study counts, provenance, evidence classification.
2. What it is / How it works / Safety signals.
3. Evidence certainty legend and outcome overview.
4. Ask Clarity as a first-class evidence interrogation tool.
5. Detailed outcome reports with evidence layers, effect estimates, and study matrices.
6. Chemistry and pharmacokinetics as a deeper technical layer.
7. Unknowns, critical appraisal, and disclosed peptide affiliate area.

## Visual refinements
- Replaced the circular score-ring treatment with a restrained evidence-classification stamp.
- Added a compact verified molecular identity strip near the compound title.
- Reduced repeated rounded cards in favor of whitespace, rules, and open report sections.
- Reduced reader-facing all-caps/monospace; mono remains for scientific values, IDs, provenance, and technical metadata.
- Simplified evidence-view controls into a compact segmented control.
- Standardized evidence colors: mechanism/lab blue, animal violet, human teal, with tier colors/textures retained.
- Simplified chemistry and pharmacokinetics into technical report sections instead of dashboard panels.

## Study inspection
- Filter by All / Human / RCT / Synthesis / Animal.
- Sort by best design or newest.
- Shows design, population, N, year, and source link.
- Displays the outcome-level evidence direction explicitly without pretending it was extracted for each individual study.

## Navigation
- Desktop Home / Research / Learn navigation is in the sticky top bar.
- Floating bottom navigation is mobile-only.
- Language switching remains available on deep Research/compound pages.

## Ask Clarity
- Moved directly after the evidence overview so it reads as a core scientific capability rather than a footer chatbot.
- Grounding/source/timing/feedback behavior is unchanged.

## Commerce
- Peptide affiliate links and disclosures remain in place.
